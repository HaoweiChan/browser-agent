About this lamp

The Aurora desk lamp is the standard model in the Aurora line, and it is built for the kind of desk that is used every day rather than photographed once. The arm is a single piece of anodised aluminium, cold-drawn and then finished with a fine bead blast, so it holds its position without the creep that plagues spring-balanced arms after a year of adjustment. The joint at the base is a friction cartridge rather than a spring, which means it is quieter, it does not sag as it ages, and it can be retensioned with a single hex key when it eventually does.

The head takes a standard bulb rather than a bonded LED array. This is a deliberate choice and it costs us a few millimetres of thickness. A bonded array is thinner and cheaper to assemble, but when it fails — and every diode array eventually fails — the lamp becomes waste. A socket means the lamp outlives the light source, and the light source is a commodity you can buy anywhere in the world for the price of a coffee.

Colour temperature is fixed by whichever bulb you fit. We ship the lamp with a 2700K bulb because that is what most people want over a desk in the evening, but the lamp is agnostic and the socket is standard. There is no application, no pairing, and no firmware. The switch is a mechanical rocker on the base, rated for fifty thousand cycles, and it is the only control on the product.

The base weighs 1.4 kilograms, which is more than the arm needs and exactly as much as the arm wants: it means you can pull the head towards you one-handed without the other hand steadying the base. The underside is faced with a moulded silicone pad, so the lamp does not travel across a glass desk and does not mark a soft one. Cable exit is at the rear left, with a moulded strain relief and two metres of braided cable.

Shipping is flat-packed in three pieces — base, arm, head — and assembly is two captive bolts and a plug. There are no adhesives in the product and no adhesives in the packaging, so all of it separates for recycling without tools. Spare friction cartridges, silicone pads and cable assemblies are stocked for ten years from the date of last manufacture, and the exploded drawing is printed inside the box rather than hidden behind a support form.

Serial prefix LAMP-SPEC. Manufactured by Nimbus Works.
